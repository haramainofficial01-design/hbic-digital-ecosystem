# HBIC Quran Academy parent tutorial — 85-second edit

Production-aligned script and edit guide. Use a real capture of `hbic.ca/quran-academy` and the signed HBIC app build for the parent screens. The user permits illustrative mockups for personal details, the QA reference and QR; label any simulated payment/check-in scene “Example” and never display a real child, card, pass or family record. Do not film or enter an actual card charge just for this tutorial.

| Time | Picture / motion | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–7 s | Phone opens the HBIC website Academy page; HBIC logo/seal, then quick view of the app Academy entry. Calm title card: “Register in minutes.” | “Assalamu alaikum. Here’s how to register your child for HBIC Quran Academy, on the website or in the HBIC app.” |
| 7–15 s | Tap “Sign in” or “Create a parent account.” Blur or mock the personal email/password. If new, briefly show the real email-verification step. | “Start at hbic.ca/quran-academy. Sign in, or create a parent account and verify your email.” |

| 27–34 s | Show the actual WhatsApp-updates choice. Later app frame shows “Enable Academy app notifications.” No invented SMS/email toggle. | “Choose WhatsApp updates, and enable app notifications after registering if you’d like class and holiday alerts.” |
| 34–47 s | Split into two clearly labeled branches. Card: select Card, accept policy/recurring authorization, tap Send registration, transition to the real Stripe-hosted Checkout page with all personal/card fields concealed. We look forward to welcoming your family. JazakumAllahu khairan.” |

Edit in 1080×1920 vertical first for WhatsApp and Instagram; keep all key text in the central 900 px safe area. Export H.264 MP4, 30 fps, AAC voice, no music. Use 0.2–0.35 s ease-in/out between scenes, restrained tap highlights and only small zooms for form controls. Captions are in the accompanying `.srt`. Keep the HBIC logo/seal visible at the opening and close, with accessible colour contrast. Website embeds may use a 1920×1080 reframed export from the same captures.

No narration audio is included: this environment has no approved natural-voice generation or recorded human narration. Record the script in a calm English voice before exporting the final MP4; do not substitute robotic or fabricated audio. Confirm the public app build and email sender status before publishing a line that implies emailed receipts or active app notifications.
