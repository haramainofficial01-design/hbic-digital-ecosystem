PRAYER ROOM TV
Masjid Bilal · Calgary

To preview on your Mac, open index.html in Chrome and press Full Screen in Settings.
On the TV, open the Internet browser and enter http://MAC-LAN-IP:8765/ while the Mac is at that address. The Mac and TV need to be on the same Wi-Fi. The Mac needs to stay on
while the page is served. If your TV has trouble with its browser, a Mac connected
by HDMI provides the same dashboard.

Use the directional buttons and OK to navigate. Back closes Settings or the
audio player. If your browser handles arrows itself, try its cursor mode.

The analog clock uses Calgary time. HBIC supplies the prayer schedule, including
local iqamah times and the Hijri date. Prayer data refreshes every five minutes.
After a connection loss, the saved schedule is shown only until it expires.
The Quran streams use MP3Quran's Ahmad Al Nufais recordings.

In Settings, Enable & preview adhan plays HBIC’s Madinah adhan excerpt by Muhammad Marwan Qassas. This
also enables playback when an adhan time arrives while the page is visible.
You need to enable it again after reopening the page. TV browser autoplay,
standby and power-saving behavior can prevent scheduled audio. This page does
not wake the TV. Try the preview through your TV speakers before relying on it.

To stop the current preview server, run launchctl remove ca.hbic.prayer-room-preview.
If it has stopped, open Terminal and run:
python3 -m http.server 8765 --directory "$HOME/Downloads/Prayer Room TV"

The Makkah and Madinah tiles open the Saudi live channels inside Prayer Room.
The broadcaster and YouTube links are available if a feed cannot play. Only
one broadcast loads at a time. Adhkar and ruqyah also have YouTube viewing links.

YouTube and Prime Video in the dock open their web destinations. Press the
Samsung Home button for the installed Smart Hub apps. The themed icons belong
to this dashboard.

See Install on Samsung TV.md for the model-specific local app workflow.
The Samsung source ZIP contains an unsigned Tizen web app. It is not a USB
installer and needs a Samsung certificate and physical TV verification.

Home shows the next Calgary prayer and its countdown. After adhan, the
countdown runs to the published iqamah and then moves to the next prayer.
Select HBIC app beside the schedule for the Masjid Bilal app page.

Choose Listen for Ahmad Al Nufais’s recitations, adhkar and ruqyah. Use the
direction keys to browse the photographic covers. Play a surah to open
Now Playing, with pause, 15-second skip and resume. Back returns to the shelf.

Home enters ambient mode after two idle minutes. Select Enter ambient mode
in Settings for immediate access. The first remote press returns to navigation.
Reduce transparency in Settings for stronger contrast. Device Reduce Motion
settings suppress focus motion.
