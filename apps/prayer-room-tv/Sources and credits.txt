PRAYER ROOM TV · SOURCES

Prayer times and Masjid Bilal assets
Hazrat Bilal Islamic Centre, Calgary. https://hbic.ca/
Live schedule: https://hbic.ca/api/display
The adhan sound is HBIC’s Madinah excerpt by Muhammad Marwan Qassas.

Live video
Saudi Broadcasting Authority’s Qur’an TV and Sunnah TV, delivered by Globecast.
Official Makkah page: https://aloula.sba.sa/live/quran
Official Madinah page: https://aloula.sba.sa/live/sunna
The built-in streams are standard definition. The dashboard renders at 4K in a
compatible browser. Samsung can scale the interface of a packaged TV app.

Qur’an and reminders
Ahmad Al Nufais audio: https://www.mp3quran.net/ar/nufais
Morning and evening adhkar, ruqyah, and Alfba’iyyat use linked reciter uploads.
The short Arabic greetings are customary greetings and supplications.
